Do something with it

Nine guided practices

A timer is the result of a practice, not the practice. These give you something specific to do — with the steps on screen before you begin, and the breath paced for you while you go.

  • Equal Breathसम वृत्ति · Sama Vritti

    Four in, four out. The plainest breath there is.

  • Alternate Nostrilअनुलोम विलोम · Anulom Vilom

    In one nostril, out the other. No retention.

  • Humming Bee Breathभ्रामरी · Bhramari

    A long humming exhale, ears gently closed.

  • Ocean Breathउज्जायी · Ujjayi

    A soft constriction at the throat, audible like a wave.

  • Cooling Breathशीतली · Sheetali

    Inhale through a curled tongue, exhale through the nose.

  • Channel Cleaningनाड़ी शोधन · Nadi Shodhana

    Alternate nostril breathing, with the breath held in.

  • Skull Shining Breathकपालभाति · Kapalabhati

    Short sharp exhales from the belly. Passive inhales.

  • Om Japaॐ जप · Om Japa

    One mala — 108 repetitions of Om, counted for you.

  • Steady Gazingत्राटक · Trataka

    Hold your gaze on one point without blinking.

The names, ratios and counts are the traditional ones. Where the classical version is not safe for a beginner, the app teaches the beginner version and says so instead of quietly swapping it. Anything with a real contraindication carries its caution above the start button — never in a footnote.

Common questions

FAQ

Is this a religious app?

No. Brahmi draws inspiration from India’s gurukul traditions, but it’s a secular self-improvement app open to anyone, of any background or belief.

Does it really work?

Brahmi gives you a structured daily practice, knowledge, and tracking to support your goal. Results depend on your consistency and circumstances, and experiences vary from person to person. It’s a tool to support your effort, not a guaranteed cure.

Why is some content locked at the start?

Because handing a beginner everything at once is how they fail at once. Articles, check-in questions and practices open as your days add up — a good part of the library is there from day one, most of it by the second week, and all of it by day thirty.

If I relapse, do I lose what I unlocked?

No. Your streak resets, but nothing re-locks. The app remembers your best run, so the reading and the practices you had earned stay open — including on the day you need them most.

Are the breathing practices safe?

The gentle ones are open from the start; the forceful and retention-based ones only arrive once you have a base under them. Every practice with a real contraindication shows its caution before you begin, not after. If you have a heart, blood-pressure, eye, or respiratory condition, or you are pregnant, please check with a doctor first.

Will you charge me automatically?

Any billing terms are shown clearly before you confirm. You’re always told what you’re signing up for, and you can manage or cancel your subscription from your app store account.

Is my data private?

Your answers and progress are personal to you. We don’t sell your personal data. We do record the calendar dates on which you open the app — the date only, never the time of day, how long you stayed, which screens you saw, or where you were — and you can see that count yourself on your profile as your active days. See our Privacy Policy and Terms for the full details.

I relapsed — did I fail?

No. Slips are part of the path, not the end of it. Brahmi is designed to help you reflect, reset, and keep going. Progress matters more than perfection.

How is this different from just a streak counter?

A counter only measures abstinence. Brahmi adds the “how” — daily practice, knowledge, and rhythm — so you’re building something, not just holding on.
